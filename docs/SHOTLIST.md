## Full descriptions

### S01 Hook: the cat (5s, dawn)

Extreme close-up. The grey tabby with the torn ear stares directly into camera, filling the frame, dawn light on one side. Only the pupils narrow and an ear twitches. At the end it blinks once and looks off frame right.

**Camera.** Locked. Camera at cat eye level.

**Audio.** Silence, then one tsukutsukuboushi cicada. A soft blink tick. No music.

### S02 The empty spot (6s, dawn)

Wide, low. A narrow Higashiyama lane at dawn: lattice, noren, potted plants, a stone gutter with water. Foreground right, the machiya door and beside it a pale rectangle on the stone where a bicycle stood, a small oil drip, one fallen persimmon leaf. Only the noren moves. Title card fades in over the held frame: 自転車 / THE BICYCLE.

**Camera.** Locked, one metre high.

**Audio.** One distant temple bell strike, sparrows, water in the stone channel. No music.

### S03 The robot wakes (6s, dawn)

Interior, dim. Close on the robot's chest: the four battery bars light up one by one with a soft chime. Eye dots blink on. He rolls to the lattice door and slides it open with a rattle; dawn light floods in.

**Camera.** Locked close, then a slow push as the door opens.

**Audio.** Power-up chime (four rising ticks), motor whir, wooden door rattle.

### S04 He sees it (6s, dawn)

From behind the robot at his height, the doorway framing the lane. The empty rectangle on the stone in front of him. Hold. His eye dots shift to a question mark. He rolls forward, looks left, looks right.

**Camera.** Locked over the shoulder.

**Audio.** Sparrows. A single soft beep. Head servo.

### S05 She looks at it (8s, dawn)

Reverse: the old woman steps into the doorway behind him with a kettle, humming three notes. She sees the spot. The hum stops mid-phrase. Her face barely changes. She looks for three full seconds. Behind her, out of focus on the genkan wall, a small framed photograph. She turns and goes inside; the door slides shut on the robot.

**Camera.** Locked, tilted up slightly from the robot's height; she is cropped at the shoulders until she leans.

**Audio.** Her hum: the first three notes of the melody, cut off. Kettle creak. Door slides shut. Silence.

### S06 The decision (7s, dawn)

Wide lane, robot alone in front of the closed door. He looks at the door, then at the spot. His screen changes from eye dots to a small bicycle glyph. He straightens his bent antenna with one gripper. He rolls off down the lane, small, slow, and the camera holds as he shrinks.

**Camera.** Locked wide, one metre.

**Audio.** Motor whir. A shopkeeper rolling up a shutter somewhere. Plucked string answers her three notes with the same three notes. Water.

### S07 Past the shiba (7s, morning)

Low tracking shot alongside the robot: doorstep pots, a stone channel, lattice. He passes a shop doorway where a red shiba lies tied to a post. The dog's head lifts, ears up, eyes following him. The robot does not notice.

**Camera.** Dolly right at the robot's speed.

**Audio.** Water in the channel, whir, the shiba's collar tag ting once.

### S08 The wall of cats (8s, morning)

A vermilion shrine wall on a stone base, morning sun. Five cats sit on top in a row, silhouetted, each a different shape. The robot rolls up below, tiny, tilts his face up and shows the bicycle glyph. The cats look down. One yawns.

**Camera.** Low and wide, slight tilt up.

**Audio.** Crows. Cicada. A cat yawn squeak.

### S09 East (7s, morning)

Close on the grey tabby. It looks at the glyph, then slowly turns its head to screen right and holds. The robot follows its gaze. The cat closes its eyes. That is all he gets.

**Camera.** Locked close, cat and robot head in frame.

**Audio.** One low plucked note. Cicada continues.

### S10 Standoff at the canal (7s, morning)

Shirakawa canal in Gion: willows, a stone bridge, water. The robot rolls along the path. Behind him the shiba follows at a distance, leash trailing. The robot stops; the shiba freezes, stiff-legged, tail curled tight, and growls, backing one step.

**Camera.** Locked wide along the canal.

**Audio.** Willow leaves, water, a low growl.

### S11 The shiba remembers (7s, morning)

The robot shows the bicycle glyph. The shiba tilts its head. A flat cutout inset appears above the dog: the green bicycle rolling past at dawn with a small figure pushing it. The shiba barks once, spins, and springs ahead. The robot follows.

**Camera.** Locked, then a small pan to follow the launch.

**Audio.** One bark, a spring-like whoosh, the melody's second fragment (five notes).

### S12 Nishiki (8s, noon)

Chapter card 昼 NOON in the corner. Nishiki market: covered arcade, stripes of coloured glass roof, stalls, and a forest of shoppers' legs at the robot's height. He navigates between legs; the shiba weaves ahead. The black cat has led them here and eats a dropped fish tail, satisfied and useless. The bobtail sits on a fish crate.

**Camera.** Slow dolly forward through the crowd at one metre.

**Audio.** Market clamour without words, plastic bag crinkle, ice, a wooden crate knock.

### S13 The cap (7s, noon)

Close on the bobtail on the crate. It is sitting on a child's yellow school cap. The robot notices. The cat will not move. The shiba barks; the cat hisses and hops off. The robot lifts the cap in his gripper and turns it over.

**Camera.** Locked close.

**Audio.** Hiss, bark, gripper servo.

### S14 Kamo river (8s, afternoon)

Wide, from the bank path. The Kamo river in afternoon gold; people sitting on the far bank evenly spaced; a heron in the shallows. The robot and shiba are small figures walking along the path. His chest shows two bars. He slows.

**Camera.** Locked very wide, long hold.

**Audio.** River. A black kite crying. Far-off children. Melody's third fragment (seven notes) on piano.

### S15 The elder cat (7s, afternoon)

Stone steps to the water. The cream long-haired elder cat sits with eyes half shut. The robot shows the glyph and holds up the cap. The cat looks at the cap, then up-river, then closes its eyes and is asleep. The shiba shoves its nose into the cap, snorts, and springs off up the path.

**Camera.** Locked medium.

**Audio.** Sniff, snort, a small bark, water.

### S16 Lanterns (7s, dusk)

Chapter card 夕 DUSK. Pontocho: a narrow alley of wooden fronts and paper lanterns. The lanterns come on one by one as the robot rolls through. Light rain begins, drawn as single lines. His wheel hits a puddle: a little puff of drops. The shiba is ahead, then further ahead.

**Camera.** Slow dolly forward.

**Audio.** Rain on paper lanterns, a train crossing bell far away, the first higurashi.

### S17 One bar (8s, dusk)

A stone canal path with red spider lilies, rain heavier, dusk violet. The robot's chest: one bar, blinking. He slows and stops under a shop awning. He sets the cap down on the step beside him. His eye dots dim. The shiba is nowhere.

**Camera.** Locked medium, rain crossing frame.

**Audio.** Rain, higurashi, the melody's first fragment played slow and minor.

### S18 The low point (9s, night)

Nearly night. The robot under the awning beside one glowing vending machine, its light in a puddle. His bicycle glyph flickers and goes out. Hold; only the rain moves. Then a soaked shiba trots in, drops a small object at his wheel, and stands panting. The eye dots come back on. He picks it up: a bicycle bell. He pushes the lever. Clack.

**Camera.** Locked, low, the vending machine the only warm light.

**Audio.** Rain thinning. Vending machine hum. A small metal clatter. Panting. Clack.

### S19 The turn (9s, night)

Rain stopped. Wet stone reflecting lanterns. The shiba springs ahead through a small vermilion gate; the robot follows with the bell. Along the walls the cats turn their heads one by one as the pair passes: grey, black, bobtail.

**Camera.** Dolly forward, faster than before.

**Audio.** Wheels on wet stone, a wet dog shake, the melody rising (nine notes) with light harmony.

### S20 The alley (9s, night)

A dead-end alley behind a shuttered shop, one bare bulb. The bicycle lies on its side, basket bent, front wheel to camera. On a step beside it a boy of about eight, no cap, knees hugged, face down, one knee scraped. The robot stops. Hold. He looks at the bicycle. Then at the boy. The boy looks up and sees a robot and a dog.

**Camera.** Locked wide at one metre; the bulb the key light.

**Audio.** Dripping. Higurashi. Silence under it.

### S21 The cap goes back (8s, night)

Close. The robot rolls to the boy and raises his gripper: the yellow cap. He places it on the boy's head, slightly crooked. His screen shows the bicycle glyph, then the glyph changes to a bicycle with a small figure pushing it and a small robot beside. The boy wipes his face. The shiba licks the scraped knee.

**Camera.** Locked close.

**Audio.** Servo, one soft beep, a tongue lick, one plucked phrase.

### S22 Walking it home (10s, night)

Wide. The boy pushes the bicycle; the robot rolls beside him at one bar; the shiba trots ahead. Lanterns, wet stone, a persimmon tree over a wall, cats in silhouette on the walls. The rear wheel squeaks once per turn.

**Camera.** Slow dolly alongside.

**Audio.** The melody, fuller but not yet complete. Wheel squeak in time. Crickets begin.

### S23 The doorstep (10s, night)

The lane at night. The old woman is sitting on the doorstep with a small lantern, waiting. She sees them and stands. The boy stops, takes off the cap, and bows deeply, holding the handlebars. Long hold. She walks to the bicycle, puts her hand on the saddle, looks at the boy. The robot hands her the bell; she screws it onto the grip.

**Camera.** Locked wide, the lantern the only warm light.

**Audio.** Bell crickets only. The bell's thread squeak. No music.

### S24 She runs (11s, night)

She pats the saddle: get on. The boy mounts, uncertain. She holds the saddle from behind and runs, an old woman running, down the lane under the lanterns. She lets go. He wobbles and keeps going. The robot watches from the step, the shiba beside him.

**Camera.** Pan left to right following the run, then hold on her, stopped, breathing.

**Audio.** THE FULL MELODY WITH HARMONY, first time. Her footsteps on stone, the wheel squeak, one breath of a laugh.

### S25 The photograph (6s, night)

Match cut. The framed photo in the genkan, in focus now: the same lane in sunlight, a younger her holding the saddle of the same green bicycle, a small girl on it, mid-wobble. Hold.

**Camera.** Locked close, slow push.

**Audio.** Melody continues, piano taking the lead.

### S26 Zero (9s, night)

Close on the robot on the step. His chest: no bars. His eye dots dim as he watches the boy ride back up the lane toward him. The screen goes dark. Her hand enters and lays a folded cloth over his dented shoulder. Off screen, the boy tries the bell: clack. Clack. Then it rings, clean and bright, once.

**Camera.** Locked close, then tilt down with her hand.

**Audio.** Melody resolves to the tonic and holds. Clack, clack, RING. Crickets.

### S27 Morning again (8s, dawn)

Same framing as S02. Dawn. The spot is not empty: the bicycle stands in it, the robot beside it with a cable running in through the lattice, one bar and rising, the shiba asleep against his wheel, the grey cat on the wall above. The noren moves. Fade to paper.

**Camera.** Locked, identical to S02.

**Audio.** Temple bell, sparrows, the hum returns: she is humming inside. Fade.

### S28 Credits (14s, paper)

Hand-lettered credits on paper: 自転車 THE BICYCLE, then 'story, drawings, music and sound by an AI in one sitting', 'for Fumiko, who taught two children to ride', small ink drawings of the cast in the margins.

**Camera.** Locked.

**Audio.** Plucked-string reprise of the melody, solo, fading.
